Real Racing 2 Controller

Phone browsers only allow tilt steering on secure pages, so the controller opens over HTTPS.

Open controller

Your browser will warn that the connection isn't private. The certificate was made by rr2fix on your Mac.

  • Safari: Show Details, then visit this website.
  • Chrome: Advanced, then Proceed.

If the controller loads but stays disconnected (some iPhones refuse WebSockets with an unverified certificate), install the rr2fix certificate, then turn it on in Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings. It can only vouch for local network addresses.
